# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree with the argument, not the person.

## Specifics that matter here

Neuron makes claims about human cognitive capability, which is a topic people
have strong and sometimes personal feelings about. Two things follow.

**Evidence beats intuition, and saying "I don't know" is a contribution.** If
you think a node's evidence band is wrong, say so with a source. If you are not
sure, say that too — this project would rather carry an honest "speculative"
than a confident guess.

**Nobody's score is a fact about them.** Neuron estimates competence from
sparse, noisy, self-selected evidence, and says so on every screen. Do not use
anyone's data — your own or a user's — to make claims about their intelligence,
and do not frame contributions in terms of who is smarter.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone's abilities or
background, sustained disruption, and publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

Open an issue if it can be discussed in public, or contact the maintainers
privately if it cannot. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may
edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate this document,
and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and PRs, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.
